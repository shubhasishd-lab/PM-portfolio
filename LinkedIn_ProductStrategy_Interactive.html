Pillar 3 — The Elevator Pitch (Step by Step)
EP
Building Your Elevator Pitch
7-step framework · Template · Examples · PM application
›

The elevator pitch is the single most important communication tool in a PM's toolkit. It crystallises your entire product strategy into 2-3 sentences that instantly communicate: who the product is for, what problem it solves, what category it belongs to, and why it wins. Built step by step from the Geoffrey Moore "Crossing the Chasm" template.

1
FOR — Target Customer
Name your specific target customer precisely
2
WHO — The Need / Opportunity
State their specific pain or unmet need
3
THE — Product Name
State your product name clearly
4
IS A — Market Category
Place your product in a known market category
5
THAT — Key Benefit
State the single most compelling benefit / outcome
6
UNLIKE — Competitive Alternative
Name what they currently use / your main competitor
7
OUR PRODUCT — Primary Differentiation
State your single most important differentiator
💡 Complete Elevator Pitch Template: "For [target customer] who [need/opportunity], [product name] is a [market category] that [key benefit]. Unlike [competitive alternative], our product [primary differentiation]." — Geoffrey Moore, Crossing the Chasm
