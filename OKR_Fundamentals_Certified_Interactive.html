Introduction to OKRs

OKRs (Objectives and Key Results) were created by Andy Grove at Intel in the 1970s and popularised by John Doerr who introduced them to Google in 1999. Today they're used by Google, Spotify, Netflix, LinkedIn, Airbnb, and thousands of other organisations globally.
