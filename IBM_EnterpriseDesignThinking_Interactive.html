The 5 Whys — Root Cause Analysis Tool
5W
The 5 Whys in Enterprise Design Thinking
Root cause · Problem framing · Deep empathy · Insight discovery
›

The 5 Whys is one of the most powerful tools in EDT — used during the Observe and Reflect stages to move past surface-level symptoms and uncover the real root cause of user problems. By asking "Why?" five times in succession, teams discover insights that completely reframe their understanding of what needs to be solved.
